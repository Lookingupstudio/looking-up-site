The Artist

Marco Benedetti

Marco is an Italian oil painter. In his private gallery he paints the musicians and the films that shaped a generation: Neil Young, Bob Marley, Syd Barrett, Kubrick, Dalí.

Every canvas is painted by hand. A new piece is added to the gallery twice a week.
