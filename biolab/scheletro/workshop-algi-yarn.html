[ The process ]

From powder to yarn, in five steps

Live session

90min

Step 01 / 05

  1. 01.

    The material

    Sodium alginate, a natural polymer from brown seaweed. What it is and why it reacts.

  2. 02.

    The mixture

    Alginate, water, glycerin and a touch of colour, blended into a smooth, even gel.

  3. 03.

    Rest

    We let the mixture rest so the air can leave. Patience is part of the material.

  4. 04.

    Extrusion

    A continuous filament into the calcium bath. It turns solid the moment it touches the water.

  5. 05.

    Drying and finishing

    Rinse, dry, keep. How to read the result: stiff, brittle or just right.
