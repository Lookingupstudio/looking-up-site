Questions

Before you book

Do I need a lab?

No. Everything happens in a normal kitchen: a few bowls, a squeeze bottle, a short list of ingredients you can order online or find in shops for molecular cooking. You get the full list after booking.

I live in another time zone. What time is the workshop?

Each date shows the time in Venice and in your own time zone. People join from Brazil, the USA, Japan, India and many other countries. If the time does not work for you, join the list: new dates are announced there first.

I have never made anything like this. Can I join?

Yes. Many participants start from zero: design and fashion students, artists, researchers, curious people. We go step by step.
